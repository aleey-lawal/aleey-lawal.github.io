## Overview
End-to-end data extraction and visualisation project comparing Tesla and
GameStop stock performance against their quarterly revenue. Used yfinance
for stock data and BeautifulSoup for revenue scraping, producing interactive
dual-axis dashboards to surface the disconnect between market price and
business fundamentals.

## Tech Stack
Python · yfinance · BeautifulSoup · Pandas · Plotly
