## Overview
Developed predictive models estimating graduate admission probability from
applicant profile data including GRE scores, TOEFL scores, CGPA, statement
of purpose strength, letter of recommendation rating, and research
experience. Compared regression and classification approaches to surface
the features most influential in admission decisions.

## Tech Stack
Python · scikit-learn · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn
