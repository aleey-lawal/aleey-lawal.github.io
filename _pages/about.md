Hi, I’m Lawal 👋

Data Scientist with a background in Economics and hands-on experience across data science, machine learning, generative AI, NLP, and data analytics. I’m interested in turning data and emerging AI technologies into practical solutions that solve real problems, improve workflows, and support better decisions.

At PPDC, I work with public-sector data for accountability research — building ETL workflows, automating data preparation, and developing interactive data products that combine anomaly detection and facility risk models to scrutinise public spending in Nigeria.

At FlexiSAF, I worked with 20,000+ textual records to support NLP-driven AI features including text clustering, summarization, and automatic insight generation.

At Rochester Institute of Technology (via Excelerate), I developed a Random Forest classification model achieving 92% accuracy and 89% F1 score for student engagement analysis.

My technical toolkit includes Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, PyTorch, NLP, Power BI, BigQuery, PostgreSQL, Git, Amazon SageMaker, and Generative AI tools.

I’m particularly interested in AI/ML applications, generative AI, responsible AI, workflow automation, and data-driven solutions in health, education, agriculture, fintech, and public-interest domains.

Open to opportunities in Data Science, AI/ML, Data Analytics, and Generative AI where I can contribute, keep learning, and build solutions with measurable impact.
