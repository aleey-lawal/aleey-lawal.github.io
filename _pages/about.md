### 👟 Global Shoe Size Predictor
A multi-output machine learning model that simultaneously predicts shoe
sizes in **EU, US, UK, JP and CN** standards from body and foot
measurements — trained on data grounded in Volumental's 1.2M foot scan
research study covering **22 countries across 5 global regions**.
